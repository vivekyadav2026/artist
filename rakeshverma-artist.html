The Artist

Hello, I'm Rakesh Verma.

I teach art the way I learned to love it — by drawing what's in front of me, over and over, until the hand stops fighting the eye. My studio sits by Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya in Ghattiya, and my students range from six‑year‑olds holding a pencil for the first time to adults returning to a hobby they set aside years ago.

Classes are small on purpose. Every student works at their own pace, on the medium that excites them, with corrections given canvas‑by‑canvas rather than as a group lecture.

  • Small batch sizes
  • Kids & adult classes
  • Beginner to advanced
  • Exam & portfolio guidance

Courses on offer

Pick a medium to begin with — most students branch into two or three over time.

01

Drawing & Sketching

Pencil control, shading, proportion and still‑life — the foundation every other course builds on.

02

Watercolour Painting

Washes, layering and control of water — for landscapes, florals and light studies.

03

Acrylic & Oil Painting

Colour mixing, texture and canvas technique, from first strokes to finished works.

04

Kids' Art Classes

Playful, age‑appropriate lessons that build confidence before technique.

05

Portrait Making

Working from photographs and live sittings to capture likeness and expression.

06

School & Exam Prep

Guided practice for school art exams, competitions and portfolio pieces.
